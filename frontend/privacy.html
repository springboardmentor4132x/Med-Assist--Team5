Privacy Policy

MedAssist AI stores only the details you give while registering and the medical history you add yourself. Passwords are stored as hashes, never as plain text.

Your profile and history can be seen only by you, by the admin, and by a doctor the admin has assigned to you. We do not share your data with anyone else.

For any question about your data, contact contact@medassist.ai.
